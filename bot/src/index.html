Troubleshooting
Won't StartConfirm the Roblox Window check is green and that you are inside HTC or GC. If global hotkeys or game input fail, re-launch as Administrator.
StoppedCheck Diagnostics for the exact last confirmed screen or detector failure. In compact mode, an operational error turns the pill red; click it to expand.

Health Timing
Health Mode
v1 scales the original narrow red probe over the target zone. v2 finds the Health bar and tracks the moving red marker into the middle of the green target.
Minimum Time Between Health F Presses
sec
Minimum gap between F presses during Health training.
Ki Detection & Timing
Ki Mode
v2 tracks the shrinking ring and is the default. Use v1 only if Ring mode cannot read the chamber background reliably; it clicks after a fixed delay instead.
Click Delay
sec
v1 only. Waits this long after a stable detection before clicking. Raise it if clicks land too early; lower it if they land too late.
Target Radius
× dot radius
v2 only. Clicks when the ring shrinks to this many dot radii. Lower clicks later, with the ring closer to the dot; higher clicks earlier. Watch the in-game Perfect/Okay/Bad: if clicks land too early, lower it; if they land after Perfect, raise it. The ring vanishes near 1.40, so lower values lose detection.
Latency Compensation
ms
v2 only. Fires the click this many ms early, based on the ring's measured shrink speed. 0 is off and suits a fast PC on low ping. Raise it in small steps if clicks keep landing late on a slower PC or a high-ping session; the ring shrinks about 10 px per 100 ms. Network ping alone is not the input delay.
Ring Brightness Min
v2 only. A pixel counts as ring when its brightest colour channel is at least this value. Default 220. Lower it if the ring looks grey or dim; raise it if bright backgrounds such as white floors cause false detections.
Ring Pixel Min
v2 only. Bright pixels needed, out of 32 sampled per radius, to count that radius as the ring. Default 6. Higher is stricter and ignores speckle; lower is more sensitive and catches partial or hidden rings.
Restart After Each Hit
Restart Health After Each F Press Beta
Off by default. Returns to the training menu and reselects Health after every F press. Confirm that progress is kept before using this for a full run.
Restart Ki After Each Circle Click Beta
Off by default. Applies to Ki Control and Ki Damage. Confirm the game credits each hit before relying on this.
Wait Before Reopening Training
sec
Shared by Health and Ki restart. Too short can cancel progress; too long removes the speed benefit.
Auto-saved. Training order and startup options apply on the next run.
